Player avatar with photo or auto-colored initials, plus optional podium ring.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Priya Anand" />
<Avatar src="/p.jpg" name="Marcus Bell" size="lg" ring="gold" />
<Avatar name="Sales Bot" size="sm" />
```

Sizes: `xs`/`sm`/`md`/`lg`/`xl`. `ring`: `gold`/`silver`/`bronze`/`brand`/`up`/`down` or any color string.
