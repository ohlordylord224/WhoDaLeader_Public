Small status pill for labels, counts, and states. Soft tones by default; `live` gives a pulsing on-air dot.

```jsx
<Badge tone="up" dot>+12%</Badge>
<Badge tone="gold">Top closer</Badge>
<Badge live>LIVE</Badge>
<Badge tone="solid-down">Slipping</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral`/`brand`/`up`/`down`/`gold`/`info` (soft) and `solid-up`/`solid-down`/`solid-gold`. Props: `dot`, `live`, `size`.
